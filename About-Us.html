------Our Value's

What We Stand For

Every decision at SweetBakes comes back to these principles.

🌿

Natural Ingredients

No artificial colours, flavours or preservatives. Real butter, real cream, real fruit — always.

🕐

Always Fresh

We bake small batches every morning. Nothing sits overnight. If it's not fresh, it doesn't go out.

👩‍🍳

Master Graft

No artificial colours, flavours or preservatives. Real butter, real cream, real fruit — always.

🌎

Community First

We source locally, compost waste, and donate leftover bakes to neighbourhood shelters every evening.

----HISTORY

Our Journey

2014

The Home Kitchen

Founder Priya Menon starts baking from her Adyar home with a borrowed stand mixer and her paati's recipes.

2016

First Storefront

Growing word-of-mouth leads to our first 400 sqft bakery on Gandhi Nagar.

2019

The Big Expansion

A 2,000 sqft production kitchen opens with professional ovens and a team of 8 bakers.

2024

50,000 Customers

A milestone — 50,000 families served. We celebrate by giving away 500 free cakes to loyal regulars.
